Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Report suspected security vulnerabilities in the school certificate portal to security@satyaminternationalschool.in. Include a clear description, affected URL or feature, impact, and steps to reproduce.

Please test only accounts and data you own or have explicit permission to access. Do not access, modify, retain, or disclose student or staff information; disrupt services; use social engineering; or publicly disclose a vulnerability before the school has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

The school will acknowledge reports and coordinate remediation where possible. This policy does not promise a reward or authorize activity that violates applicable law.
